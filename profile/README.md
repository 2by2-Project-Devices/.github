This organization serves as a research and development space for AOSP device bring-up and related device engineering by [2by2 Project](https://github.com/2by2-Project).

Repositories hosted here may contain experimental, incomplete, or research-oriented device trees and other development resources. Devices that become sufficiently stable and maintainable may later be adopted by downstream projects.

> **Reference repositories**
>
> Repositories tagged with `reference` are intended primarily for research and documentation purposes. They may relate to devices using non-official unlocking methods or to ongoing device investigations.
>
> For security reasons, unlock procedures, exploit details, and bypass instructions are intentionally not published.
